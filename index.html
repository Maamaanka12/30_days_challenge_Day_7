About

A school run on written timetables, not portals within portals.

Brookfield opened in 1998 as a single building with 120 pupils. Today we keep the same rule: any parent can read the full week timetable, the grading scale, and the term calendar without a login.

  1. Fixed weekly timetable. Periods 1 to 6 run 8:30 a.m. to 3:10 p.m. Clubs run 3:30 p.m. to 4:30 p.m.
  2. Friday grade post. Scores appear in the parent letter each Friday with teacher comments.
  3. Two adviser meetings per term. Each pupil reviews targets with the same adviser in October and February.
  4. Library open late. The reading room stays open until 6:00 p.m. on school days.

Fees, 2026

Tuition is $0 as a public charter school. Bus pass is $180 per year. Lunch is $3.10 per day, free for eligible families. Supply list totals about $45.

Terms of use

1. This site gives general school information. Timetables and dates can change; the printed letter sent home is the final record.

2. Application data is used only to process admission. We keep it for 12 months, then delete it.

3. Do not post unlawful or harmful content through any form on this site.

Privacy policy

We collect names and contact details entered in forms. We use them to answer enquiries and process applications. We do not sell personal data. Contact privacy@brookfield.example to request access or deletion. This policy was updated August 1, 2026.
